---

Call the Skill tool with "interview".

The idea must fit in this session. If it grows past that (many independent parts, new infrastructure, research needed first, several sessions of work), propose trimming it or switching to `main-quest`.

When the interview is done, write a card in the chat and confirm before doing anything:

```
## 🗡️ Side quest: <name>

**Goal**: <one sentence>
**Expected result**: <what exists when it's done>
**Done when**: <observable criteria>
**Decisions**: <short list>
**Prerequisites**: <✅/⚠️/❌ checklist>
**Plan**: <3-7 steps, each leaves something checkable>
**Out of scope**: <what we decided not to do>
```
